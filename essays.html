  • The Depreciation of Knowledge September 2026
    ▾

    A short essay on the value of knowledge in a world with Artificial Superintelligence. It is a reflection attempting to address the question: what happens when knowledge become instantaneously accessible? I try to take a philisophical and economic approach to this question. The essay is neither an argument supporting nor criticizing AI, but rather a reflection on the implications of its existence. Just a thought.
