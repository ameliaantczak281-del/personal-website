  • The Depreciation of Knowledge September 2026
    ▾

    A short essay discussing the value of knowledge in a world with Artificial Superintelligence. It is a reflection attempting to address the question: What happens when knowledge becomes instantaneously accessible? In the essay, I try to take a philosophical and economic approach to this question. I am neither a philosopher nor an economist, but I think it is an interesting perspective to consider when looking at the future of AI. Please note that I am not supporting nor opposing the development of AI, but rather sharing my thoughts on the implications of its development. I hope you enjoy reading it.
