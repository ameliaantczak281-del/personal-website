What's up I’m Millie.

I'm in high school. I play softball pretty competitively, but I also really like outdoor activities. I love to fish, hike, ski, camp, surf, and climb. I also love to chill with my friends and eat food. I like cookies, chocolate, steak, and ice cream, but not together.

I built this website because I wanted somewhere to share my thoughts with the world. It's no fun to keep them to yourself. I think sharing ideas, irrespective of whether they are good, bad, right, or wrong, is how we can progress. I think the exchange of opinions is amazing and undervalued. I also think society has become increasingly polarized and hypercritical, which has caused fear around being vulnerable and sharing your ideas. This is a stab at that.
